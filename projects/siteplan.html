Family Roots Explorer Site Plan

Site Name

Family Roots Explorer

Family Roots Explorer was chosen because it focuses on helping people discover their family origins, learn about their ancestors, and begin building their family trees.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to introduce visitors to family history and genealogy research. The site will explain the importance of learning about ancestors, provide guidance on creating a family tree, share tips for finding historical records, and offer resources that help beginners start their family history journey.

Scenarios

  • How do I start building my family tree if I know very little about my ancestors?
  • What records and resources can help me find information about my relatives?
  • How can I preserve family stories and photographs for future generations?

Color Schema

  • Dark Blue (#1D3557) - Used for headings, navigation, and footer.
  • Light Gray (#F1F5F9) - Used for the page background.
  • Teal (#457B9D) - Used for buttons, links, and accents.
  • White (#FFFFFF) - Used for content sections.

Typography

  • Poppins - Used for headings and titles.
  • Roboto - Used for paragraphs, lists, and body text.
