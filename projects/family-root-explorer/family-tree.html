How to Build a Family Tree

A family tree is a diagram that shows relationships between family members. Start with yourself, then add your parents, grandparents, and other relatives.

Steps to Get Started

  1. Talk to parents and grandparents.
  2. Write down names and important dates.
  3. Collect photographs and documents.
  4. Organize the information into a family tree.
  5. Continue researching older generations.
